# Spaghetti al Pomodoro

## Description

Spaghetti al pomodoro is an Italian pasta dish with tomato, garlic, olive oil, and basil—a simple, flavorful lunch.

Its history is linked to Naples, Italy. Ippolito Cavalcanti’s nineteenth-century cookbook documented vermicelli with tomato sauce, an early example of this combination.

Historical source: [Academia Barilla](https://www.barilla.com/global/help-with/gastronomic-library/theoretical-practical-cuisine)

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 400 g dried, egg-free spaghetti
- [ ] 800 g canned crushed tomatoes
- [ ] 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 8–10 fresh basil leaves
- [ ] Salt, to taste
- [ ] Black pepper, to taste
- [ ] Water, for boiling pasta

### Steps for preparation:

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat.
2. Add garlic and cook for 30–60 seconds without browning.
3. Stir in tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer gently for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
5. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions until al dente—tender with a slight bite.
6. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water, then drain.
7. Toss spaghetti with the sauce over low heat for 1–2 minutes. Add reserved water gradually if needed.
8. Stir in torn basil, taste for seasoning, and serve immediately.

### Notes:

- Do not rinse cooked spaghetti.
- Cook the pasta while the sauce simmers.
- Add chili flakes for optional heat.
