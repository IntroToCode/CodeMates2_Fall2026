# One-Pot Three-Bean Chili

## Description

This vegetarian chili combines kidney beans, black beans, and pinto beans with tomatoes and peppers in one pot. Serve it with rice and let everyone add their own toppings for an easy dinner for a group of six.

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 2 tablespoons olive oil
- [ ] 1 large onion, diced
- [ ] 2 bell peppers, seeded and diced
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- [ ] 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 teaspoon mild chili powder
- [ ] 1 x 800 g can crushed tomatoes
- [ ] 1 x 400 g can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 1 x 400 g can black beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 1 x 400 g can pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 150 g frozen or drained canned corn
- [ ] 400 ml vegetable broth
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- [ ] 300 g uncooked rice, to serve
- [ ] Water for the rice, according to its package instructions
- [ ] Optional toppings: 1 diced avocado, 1 lime cut into wedges, and 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro

### Steps for preparation:

1. Drain and rinse the three cans of beans. Dice the onion and peppers, and mince the garlic.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and peppers, and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Add the garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and mild chili powder. Stir for 1 minute.
4. Add the crushed tomatoes, beans, corn, vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.
5. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the peppers are tender and the chili has thickened. Add a splash of water if it becomes too thick.
6. While the chili simmers, cook the rice with water according to its package instructions, so it is ready at the same time as the chili.
7. Taste the chili and adjust the salt. Prepare the optional toppings in separate small bowls.
8. Divide the rice and chili among six bowls, or set out both pots for everyone to serve themselves. Offer the toppings separately.

### Notes:

- Use a pot with a capacity of at least 4 liters so there is room to stir.
- The canned bean weights refer to the unopened cans; each yields roughly 240 g of drained beans.
- Keeping the chili mild and offering hot sauce separately lets each person choose their own heat level.
- To double the recipe, double every measured ingredient, use a larger pot, and cook until the peppers are tender. Cooking time does not automatically double.
